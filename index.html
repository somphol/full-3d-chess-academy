    Academy handbook

    How the studio works

    Play

    Click a piece, then a glowing square. Legal moves appear as rings. Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom, right-drag to pan.

    Inspect

    Click any piece to lift it, see its value and movement rules, and project its paths across the board.

    Undo & learn

    When the guard spots a hanging piece or a mate-in-one, it explains the threat and offers a clean undo.

    Puzzles

    Three beginner drills load a position onto the board. Find the move; the coach explains why it works.

    Keys

    U undo · N new game · I inspect lens · L language · 1/2/3 camera · Esc deselect.

    Your data

    Games and streaks live in this browser's localStorage only. Export PGN or JSON any time; nothing is sent anywhere.

    Built with Three.js, the OpenCode agent harness, and the DeepSeek V4.1 Flash model.

    Serving over HTTP is recommended. Three.js loads from jsDelivr, so the studio needs a connection the first time.
